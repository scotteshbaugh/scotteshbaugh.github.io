Context -- statement with a numbered list

How might we design a better way for admins to configure a self-service experience?
IAM platforms perform many functions and Self-service is a foundational element. Out-of-the-box self-service experiences are often limited in functionality and lag in usability. Customers tend to build their own, but are left with the burden of maintaining them. The vision was to make the self-service experience highly configurable and consumer grade.
