Self-service portal

A cross-device self-service experience, shaped by administrator settings for an IAM platform.

The capability needed to be configurable, brandable, and balance consumer-grade usability with enterprise requirements. The platform now has a self-service experience aligned with its capabilities and supports complex configuration without compromising usability. I led the design for the Self-Service experience and two upstream application areas that it relied on. The internal client for this project was Thales' Identity and Access Management (IAM) organization. The engagement intermittently across 11 months in 2025.

Context

How might we design a better way for admins to configure a self-service experience? IAM platforms perform many functions and Self-service is a foundational element. Out-of-the-box self-service experiences are often limited in functionality and lag in usability. Customers tend to build their own, but are left with the burden of maintaining them. The vision was to make the self-service experience highly configurable and consumer grade.
